Drop your real photos in this folder with the exact base names below.
Any file extension works — .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp — the site tries
each one in that order and uses whichever it finds. Anything still
missing falls back to a placeholder automatically, so the site never
breaks while you're still collecting photos.

There are three separate photo sets, each its own pictures — nothing
is reused between them.

HER JOURNEY  (screen 4 — the still frame behind each video, before she
taps play)
  journey1   14 Sept 2003   "The Beginning"                    — baby photo
  journey2   2008           "Small, and Completely in Charge"  — toddler / little kid
  journey3   2014           "School Days"                      — school years
  journey4   2020           "Corona Pandemic"                  — pandemic-era photo
  journey5   2021           "Golden College Days"              — college years
  journey6   25 Jan 2026    "The Day You Walked In"            — around when you met, or a recent favourite

A LITTLE LOOK BACK  ("the last twenty-three years, in a few frames" —
the auto-flashing montage screen just before The End)
  frames1 … frames6   any six photos spanning childhood to today —
                      completely separate from the journey1–6 set above,
                      pick whatever tells the story best on its own

OUR FAVOURITE MEMORIES  (scratch card 3, opened from "Five Little Secrets")
  memory1    "Our first date"
  memory2    "That trip we still talk about"
  memory3    "Just us, being silly"

Sizing:
  journey1–6   portrait or square photos work best — shown "contained"
               (never cropped, just letterboxed if needed) behind each
               video.
  frames1–6    portrait or square — these are "cover-cropped" to a tall
               4:5 frame, so a wide landscape photo will lose its sides.
  memory1–3    portrait-ish, roughly 3:4.

The years, chapter titles and captions are all editable in
js/script.js — search for "JOURNEY_CHAPTERS" for the journey screen,
or "cardContent" for the scratch-card text.
