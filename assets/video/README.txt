Two kinds of video go in this folder. All are optional — until you add
them, each one falls back to a small public sample clip automatically,
so nothing ever looks broken while you're still collecting footage.

HER JOURNEY (screen 4 — one video per chapter, watched on request)
  journey1.mp4   2003      a clip from when she was a baby
  journey2.mp4   2008      a clip from when she was little
  journey3.mp4   2014      a clip from her school years
  journey4.mp4   2019      a clip from her teenage years
  journey5.mp4   Feb 2026  a clip from around when you met
  journey6.mp4   Today     a recent favourite clip

Each chapter pauses and shows a "watch" button — it only plays if she
taps it, and she can always tap "continue" instead. Keep clips short
(10-30s) so the pacing stays tight. Portrait or landscape both work;
the player crops to fill the frame (object-fit: cover).

FINAL SURPRISE (screen 7)
  birthday-wish.mp4   your own recorded "happy birthday" message or a
                       cartoon clip — this is the big one at the end.

Free, downloadable clips: Pixabay Videos, Mixkit, or Pexels (search
"happy birthday" / whatever moment you need) — download the mp4,
rename it exactly as above, and drop it in this folder.
