Three audio files go in this folder. All are optional — the site works
without them and never shows an error if they're missing.

  song.mp3
      Soft romantic instrumental. Loops quietly in the background from
      the moment she opens the envelope — there's no visible on/off
      button by design, it just plays if the file exists and stays
      silent (no error, no broken UI) if it doesn't.

  birthday-song.mp3
      The happy-birthday song. Plays automatically the moment she blows
      out the candles, and stops after exactly 10 seconds. The background
      instrumental pauses while it plays and resumes afterwards.

  blast.mp3
      A short confetti pop / whoosh sound. Plays every time the confetti
      cannons fire — opening the letter, blowing out the candles, and
      the other big reveal moments. Keep it SHORT (well under a second
      is ideal) since it can fire several times in quick succession.

Good free sources: Pixabay Music/SFX, YouTube Audio Library (filter by
"no copyright"), Mixkit Sound Effects (search "confetti" or "pop") for
blast.mp3 specifically. Keep filenames exactly as above.
